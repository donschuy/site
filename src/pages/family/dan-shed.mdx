   text: "The corner blocks are called Camo blocks. Available at Home Depot. Make leveling the foundation a lot easier. Dug down 4-6 inches, threw a bag of gravel in the hole (drainage), tamped it down then leveled the Camo block. I was able to set the four blocks, level the sleepers, then frame the platform yesterday in about 5 hours. Squared the frame and secured it to the sleepers, then cut the support blocks for the insulation foam this morning in between lightning storms (and rain squalls). Installed the foam this afternoon. The insulation will help form a water resistant barrier which should lengthen the life of the floor. At least that’s what the internet, and plans, said."
