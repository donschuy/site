# Hmong New Year Celebration in Seattle Center
*November 14, 2026*

Today, a Saturday, Ying and I are going to the Seattle Center to meet her young cousin and family at a Hmong New Year celebration. The two school age daughters will be performing in a dance as one of the presentations in the celebration.

Ying and I arrived early so we took some time to visit the center. No time to go in, but we took pictures of the [MOPOP (Museum of Pop Culture)](https://www.mopop.org), the [Space Needle](https://www.spaceneedle.com) of course, and the
[Pacific Science Center](https://pacificsciencecenter.org).
