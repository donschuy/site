# guy with hat: escape from rising tides
*September 22, 2026*

Charlie wrote another game. Click here to check it out: [guy with hat: escape from rising tides](https://krankdud.itch.io/guy-with-hat-escape-from-rising-tides)

This game was created for [GBJAM14](https://itch.io/jam/gbjam-14). Charlie created his game submission to the contest in a week. See the page for the
context rules, but briefly it had to be Gameboy like and a secondary theme of the game should be "old gold".

Here is the title screen:

![](/guy-with-hat/gwt-title.png)

The player instructions are as follows:

*You are the guy with hat. You've found some gold in an ancient temple filled with mummies, bats, and ghosts. Uh oh, looks like the gold was a trap and now it's flooding with water! Get out of there, guy with hat!*

On the keyboard you can use Z to jump and arrow keys to move around. You can also raise the water faster with the X key.

I paused the game while playing to capture a couple screen shots.

The guy with the hat needs to jump on the platforms before the rising water overtakes him.
