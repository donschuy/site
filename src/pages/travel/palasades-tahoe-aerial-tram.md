# Palasades Tahoe Aerial Tram Lift
*September 2, 2026*

[Palasades Tahoe Aerial Tram](https://www.palisadestahoe.com/events-and-activities/activity-finder/aerial-tram) page.

The weather in the Lake Tahoe area got windy and cold so we opted to skip doing a hike toxay and instead visit the Palasades Tahoe Aerial Tram as an alternative activiyy.

The cost was $41 per person. The ticket allows you to go up multiple times if you like.
The restaurant at High Camp up on top was closed as were other activities. As it is off season, there wasn't a lot going on but the view was what we were after.

Palasades Tahoe was the site of the 1960 Olympics.
