# Sacramento, Capitol Mall
*July 25, 2026*

[Wikipedia - Capitol Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Mall) for historic details.

My wife and I were visiting family in Sacramento. We stayed at a hotel that was within walking distance of the Capitol Mall and took the
opportunity for a brief walk to admire the California State Capitol building and its surroundings.

The trees were huge. Ying is standing next to one for scale.
