# Logan Shoals Vista Point, Lake Tahoe
*August 30, 2026*

[Tahoe Trail Guide](https://tahoetrailguide.com/hiking-logan-shoals-vista-point/) page for this "hike".

Logan Shoals Vista Point is sort of just that, a vista point you see a sign for on the highway. We stopped to get a few pictures and found that there
was more to it as below there is a nice hiking trail that goes along the lake. We didn't pursue the trail, but found a potential activity for the next
time we visit Lake Tahoe.

Here are some photos we took during our short stay before we jumped back into the car to continue down the road.
