# Calawee Cove Beach via the Rubicon trail
*July 28, 2026*

[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/rubicon-trail--15) and [related page](https://www.alltrails.com/poi/us/california/south-lake-tahoe/calawee-cove-beach) for this hike.

This was our second hike in the Lake Tahoe area. We planned on doing a third, but some of us (including me) were too blistered and sore to take on
another more aggressive hike.

We start at Emerald Bay State Park.

The young men take the lead again and we see them on occasion when they stop and rest allowing us to catch up.
