# Walupt Creek Falls
*August 20, 2026*

[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/walupt-creek-falls--2) for this hike.

Ying has been pretty excited about exploring this hike after reading about it. It is a 3 1/2 hour drive for us to get to the trailhead, but we
feel it was definitely worth it.

The trail doesn't have an official trailhead, but it wasn't hard to find by following the instructions of other hikers.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4401.webp)

Someone left some pink ribbons to help people know where the actual trail is. We found the trail to be well traveled so it wasn't too
difficult except that sometimes there would be a fork in the trail and it wasn't clear what was the real trail and what wasn't.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4403.webp)

This piece of wood against the fallen tree is an attempt to tell hikers this isn't the way to go.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4405.webp)

There are a few places where walking across or over logs and ducking under branches are necessary.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4407.webp)

Basically, once you cross the creek, the trail will be following it down to the waterfalls. Sometimes the trail is up high above the water;
other times you are close and can see smaller waterfalls along the way.

everything once we arrive at the bottom of the falls.

Finally at the bottom. Here there is the Cispus river that the water from the falls joins.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4465.webp)

The cliffs are steep and magnificant from down here. I believe the water carved these out over time.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4466.webp)

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4467.webp)

Our first view of the falls from the bottom. We notice that unfortunately for pictures, a large part of the falls is in the shade. Also, we knew
that we are later in the year when there isn't as much water coming down. If you look online you will see pictures where there is a lot more
water. We will time it better next time, but it still is a great treat to see and we are glad we made the effort.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4469.webp)

Ying goes barefoot. I have some water shoes in my pack and use them. We know from trail reports that you can partially walk up the
lower part of the falls. It is slippery so we have to be careful.

The water is cold at first but it quickly becomes comfortable in the hot sun.

![](/walupt-creek-falls-august-2026/IMG_4470.webp)

Most of the rocks are covered in a dark green moss. This actually helps a lot with walking on it. We found that where the moss isn't growing, the
rocks are very slippery and its not safe to walk there.
