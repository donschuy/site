# Mount Tallac Trail, Lake Tahoe
*August 31, 2026*

[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/mt-tallac-trail) for this hike.
Other site links about this trail: [Visit Lake Tahoe](https://visitlaketahoe.com/things-to-do/hikes/mount-tallac-trail/) page,
[Tahoe Trail Guide](https://tahoetrailguide.com/hiking-mount-tallac-via-mount-tallac-trailhead/) page,
[West Coast Wayfarers](https://westcoastwayfarers.com/mt-tallac-hike-guide/) page.

This hike is around 10 miles in length; approximately 5 miles in and 5 miles back. The pages linked above seem to disagree on length; All Trails says
it is 9.5 miles round trip.

The elevation gain is 3,284 feet, you are 9,735 feet from sea level at the top. We found the hike challenging for our group, but younger hikers were
moving along a lot more quickly. As it was, we ended up finishing up the last part of the trail by flashlight.

I took around 340 pictures and edited them down to 90, which is still a lot for a single web page. You may want to scroll past the photos that you find
less interesting.

We got down to alongside Falling Leaf Lake before we needed our phone flashlights. The others had more battery left and my wife had
an battery pack to suppliment. We found we could hike the rest of the way out by flashlight at pretty much the same pace as we could
in daylight.

We hiked about a half hour to an hour in the dark. Next time, we will do better at making sure we get out before dark.

We all enjoyed an exceptional hike and good time.
