# Lake Genevieve and Lake Crag
*July 27, 2026*

[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/lake-genevieve-and-crag-lake-via-meeks-bay-trail) for this hike.
[Outbound page](https://www.theoutbound.com/california/hiking/meeks-bay-trail-to-lake-genevieve-and-crag-lake) for this hike.

We joined my wife's cousin and her family on a hike in the Lake Tahoe area. The trailhead is called Meeks Bay Trail. From there
the trail goes to our main destination, Lake Genevieve, and then shortly afterwards Lake Crag.

Ying completes the day hiker registration at the trailhead sign.

![](/lake-genevieve/IMG_3318.webp)

We are off. The young men in our party will lead and occasionally wait for us to catch up. The first part of the hike was under some restoration construction.

![](/lake-genevieve/IMG_3320.webp)

We saw workers, log piles, sawdust piles and for a short while it was a very dusty road rather than a trail.

![](/lake-genevieve/IMG_3321.webp)

The early part of the hike was open as previously burnt trees were being removed.

![](/lake-genevieve/IMG_3323.webp)

We are on a normal trail now, with some uphill.

![](/lake-genevieve/IMG_3332.webp)

![](/lake-genevieve/IMG_3341.webp)

This area has tremendous rocks. I end up taking lots of photos like this along the trails on this hike and the one we did the following day.

![](/lake-genevieve/IMG_3346.webp)

In our party we have [Stan Andrew Vang](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm4886783/), an actor and filmmaker based in Los Angeles. Like the rest of his family, he was
quite cheerful and a wonderful person to be with.
