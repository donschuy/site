# Kendall Katwalk
*October 8, 2026*

[Washington Trails Association page](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/kendall-katwalk) for this hike.
[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/kendall-katwalk-trail--3) for this hike.

This was a long hike, 8 1/2 hours with lots of picture taking on the way up. It took us around 5 hours to go to the
two lakes beyond the katwalk and around 3 hours to get back. We took a lunch break at the katwalk and had a nice
chat with some fellow hikers.

The hike is rated hard on the WTA page. I think that is primarily because of its length. There are no super steep
climbs, however, the hike is consistently uphill, just at a reasonable rate of climb so it isn't too difficult
other than of course being a really long day hike.
The hike was not crowded though we did see some folks from time to time. A few times we saw folks running it;
that would certainly be a good workout for a healthy person that could manage it.

The map at the trailhead shows the route from where you start to the Katwalk, and then to the two lakes; Gravel Lake and Ridge Lake.
