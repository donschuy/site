# Mount Rainier, Paradise - Fall Colors
*October 1, 2026*

[Washington Trails Association page](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/skyline-trail-loop) for this hike.
[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/skyline-trail) for this hike.

Ying has been anxious to get out and do some hiking while the fall colors are present. Skyline Trail Loop is exceptional in the fall. We headed out at 6 AM as the drive to Paradise is about 2 1/2 hours
plus time for the usual coffee stop. We knew that we might not get to see the mountain today as the clouds could completely cover it.

It is a Thursday but the trail reports from the day before spoke of the park being crowded. We we got there around 9 AM and found there was plenty of parking still. This is Ying's boss BMW X5 in a nearly
empty lower parking lot.
![](/skyline-october-2026/IMG_6950.webp)

Looking back at the visitor center as we start up the trail.

Pretty much immediately we get to see what we came for. We were glad we didn't come too late in the year and miss it. A lot of folks will visit Paradise today for the same thing. Fortunately, you don't
have to be a hiker in order to enjoy this as there is a lot to see within short walking distance for the non-hikers.

Online, Paradise is getting some flak in hiker reports for the rude behavior of some folks on the trail. We found out that early in the day it is quite the opposite. On our way up we meet a lot of really
nice folks enjoying the hike and sights with camaraderie. As the day goes on there are more folks with maybe less hiking experience. My recommendation is to hit the trail early and be one with the serious
nature lovers!

visible in the lower left that is [Wilson Glacier Falls](https://www.nps.gov/places/wilson-glacier-falls.htm?utm_source=place&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=experience_more&utm_content=small). Today you can actually see the water flowing rather than just see the white lines.

We (as most folks) recommend taking the Skyline Loop Trail clockwise. You get to look up to the mountain as you hike up. If you go counter-clockwise the mountain is often hidden from view
on the way up. Going clockwise, you get to see some spectacular views on the way down.
