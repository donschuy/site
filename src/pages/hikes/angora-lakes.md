# Angora Lakes Trail
*September 1, 2026*

[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/angora-lakes-trail) for this hike.
[Visit Lake Tahoe](hhttps://maps.visitlaketahoe.com/en/hikes/resource/angora-lakes-trail#1788400735480) page.

This is an easy short hike. However Steve, as our hike leader and guide, recommended we park long before the trail head as the road along the top of a
ridge offers beautiful scenery which is arguably better than the designated hike itself. The road has red lines indicating where you are not allowed to
park, but there were plenty of places to park.
