# Hike to Colchuck Lake, Leavenworth
*September 7, 2026*

[Washington Trails Association page](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/colchuck-lake) for this hike.
[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/colchuck-lake-via-stuart-lake-trail) for this hike.

This is a tough hike, at least for me. The round trip according to Ying's watch was 10.77 miles and there were sections with a lot of elevation gain. As you will see from the photos, it
certainly was worth the effort.

The WTA page says it is 8 miles round trip. Maybe that doesn't include walking to the far side of the lake. The All Trails page measures it as 9.1 miles. There was 4 miles of dirt road
with the early parts having deep potholes, so while we did see sedans at the trailhead, use caution. Always read trip reports from the hiking sites before
attempting a hike so that you come prepared.

This page has over 90 pictures on it as I want to represent what it was like throughout the hike. Feel free to scroll past photos you are less intrested in.
