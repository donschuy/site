# Park Butte Lookout
*August 21, 2026*

[Washington Trails Association page](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/park-butte) for this hike.
[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/park-butte-lookout-trail) for this hike.

This one is an 8 mile round trip. We did this on a Friday and it wasn't overly crowded which was surprising for it being a favorite of many.
This was the third time for me and the fourth for my wife, Ying. She did the hike one year with our son Charlie while I was at work. Now that
I'm retired I can go on hikes any day of the week.

The long dirt road to the trailhead is full of significant pot holes that had our truck swaying left and right. You would have to take this
carefully or not at all if your vehicle doesn't have high clearance. That said, we did see some small cars at the trailhead showing that some
folks are willing to brave it.

The prize at the hike destination is a lookout tower that is maintained by a hiker's association, the Skagit Alpine Club, according to the sign
on the structure. However, as you will see from the photos, this hike has a lot to enjoy along the way.
