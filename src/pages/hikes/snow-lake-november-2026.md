# Snow Lake in November
*November 12, 2026*

[Washington Trails Association page](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/snow-lake) for this hike.
[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/snow-lake-trail) for this hike.

Ying and I decided to check out Snow Lake today. We imagined it would be quite different than what it is in the summer.

![](/snow-lake-2/IMG_8355.webp)

We dressed with extra clothes as it was initially pretty cold until we worked up some sweat from the hike.

![](/snow-lake-2/IMG_8356.webp)

![](/snow-lake-2/IMG_8358.webp)

It was quite foggy.

![](/snow-lake-2/IMG_8359.webp)

![](/snow-lake-2/IMG_8360.webp)
