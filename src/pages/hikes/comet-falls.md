# Mount Rainier, Comet Waterfall
*October 1, 2026*

[Washington Trails Association page](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/comet-falls) for this hike.
[All Trails page](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/washington/comet-falls-via-van-trump-trail) for this hike.

This hike is considered "moderate" in difficulty but it can be a challenge if you are doing it same day immediately after the [Skyline Loop Trail](https://donschuy.com/hikes/skyline-october-2026/) as we did.
I suppose that depends on your fitness level. A hiker we spoke to on the trail said that he ran into many folks who had just done that. This is a short hike but there is some steep climbing. It took us about
2 1/2 hours to complete it and that was with picture taking at the falls.

We saw a warning about bears posted at the trailhead sign. We always carry a canister of bear spray but haven't run into a bear on the trails yet.
